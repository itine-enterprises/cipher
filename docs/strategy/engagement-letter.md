# Studio Exit engagement letter (template, one page)

Not legal advice. Have a lawyer read this once before the first paid migration.

**Parties.** [Your legal name / entity] ("Studio Exit") and [Studio legal name] ("the Studio").

**Service.** [Exit Audit at $199 / Full Migration at $X / Studio Ops at $Y per month], as described
in the attached report. Any work outside that description is quoted separately in writing.

**What the Studio does.** Sends the non-renewal notice to Mindbody from the account owner's
email, using the draft we provide. Adds Studio Exit as a staff user on Mindbody and on the
new platform with the permissions listed in the plan, and removes that access at completion.
Requests any paid data export from Mindbody in its own name. Sends all messages to its clients
from its own accounts. Clears stored card data in Mindbody through its own staff user, on the
date in the plan.

**What Studio Exit does not do.** Does not sign, send, or negotiate anything with Mindbody or
any vendor on the Studio's behalf. Does not receive, store, or transmit card numbers. Does not
message the Studio's clients from its own accounts. Does not give legal advice.

**Data.** Studio Exit receives client exports solely to perform the service, keeps them on
encrypted storage, deletes them within 30 days after cutover, and never uses them for any
other purpose. The Studio remains the controller of its client data.

**Timing and billing.** Mindbody bills to the end of the Studio's term regardless of usage. The
plan is timed to that date. Studio Exit does not guarantee the absence of overlapping charges,
platform behaviour, or vendor acceptance of any data export.

**Referral fees.** Where a recommended platform pays Studio Exit a referral fee, the amount is
disclosed in the report. The fee does not determine the recommendation.

**Guarantee.** For the Exit Audit only: if the report identifies less than $1,500 a year in
savings and no exit window the Studio did not already know about, the $199 is refunded on
request.

**Liability.** Studio Exit's total liability under this engagement is capped at the fees paid
for it. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

**Term.** Either party may end the engagement in writing; fees for work completed remain due.
Studio Ops is month to month and cancels with 30 days' notice.
